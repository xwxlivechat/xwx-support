How can we help?

Welcome to the official XWX support page. We're here to help you enjoy connecting with people through conversations, messaging, and voice rooms.

Account and Login

For help signing in or accessing your account, contact our support team using the contact information provided in the app or on this page.

Voice Rooms and Messaging

If voice rooms, messages, or other features aren't working, try updating the app and checking your internet connection. If the problem continues, contact support and describe the issue.

Safety and Reporting

If you encounter abusive behavior or inappropriate content, use the reporting and blocking features available in the app, where provided.

Contact Support

For assistance, contact the XWX support team through the support contact listed in the app. Please do not send passwords or verification codes.

We will add a dedicated support email here once it is available.
