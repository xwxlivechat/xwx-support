XWX Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XWX ("we," "our," or "the app") handles information when you use the XWX social networking application.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, XWX may process information you provide, such as account and profile details, messages, and content you choose to share. Technical information may also be processed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide app features, support communication between users, maintain account security, troubleshoot issues, enforce community rules, and respond to support requests.

3. Messages and Voice Rooms

Messages and voice-room features may process the information needed to deliver communications. Do not share sensitive personal information in public areas or with people you do not trust. Whether communications are stored or recorded depends on the features and services actually implemented in the app.

4. Service Providers

XWX may rely on third-party service providers to operate features such as hosting, authentication, messaging, or voice communication. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services. We will identify the relevant providers and practices in this policy as applicable.

5. Sharing Information

Information may be visible to other users when you choose to share it through social features. Information may also be processed by service providers or disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

6. Retention and Security

Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use appropriate safeguards for the service, but no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You should be able to request help with your account or privacy concerns through the support contact provided in the XWX app. Available options for accessing, correcting, or deleting information depend on the features implemented and applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

XWX is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the XWX support team.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the XWX support team through the support contact available in the app. A dedicated contact method should be published here before the app is submitted for review.
